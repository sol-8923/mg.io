의견 묻고, 말하고, 공감하기 p.122~126

AWhat do you think about the board game?
BI think it's fun.
AI feel the same. I can enjoy it with my friends.

꼭 외울 3가지

  • 묻기What do you think about ~?
  • 말하기I think it's ~. (I think + 주어 + 동사)
  • 공감I feel the same. 뒤에 이유를 덧붙인다
상황표현
동의하지 않을 때I don't think so. + 자기 의견 덧붙이기 (p.125)
추측하며 말하기I think it's a cup. / I think it's a flower pot.
대화 속 다른 표현What about you? / I like it, too. / That's interesting!
※ think 뒤가 아니라 about 뒤에 의견 대상이 온다. (p.123, 126)

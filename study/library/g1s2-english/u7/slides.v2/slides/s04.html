Different Looks (p.125)

Fun Fun Story (p.126)

  • What do you think about my house? → I think it's beautiful!
  • The cat is scary. → I feel the same.
  • 묻기=What do you think about the book?, 말하기=I think it's boring., 공감=I feel the same.

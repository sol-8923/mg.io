언어 형식 1 · 부정사(명사적 용법)

목적어 자리의 to부정사 p.96~97

형태와 뜻 (decide, agree, expect도 같은 쓰임)

to + 동사원형이 동사의 목적어로 쓰여 ~하는 것(을), ~하기(로)

동사뜻
want to ~~하기를 원하다
hope to ~~하기를 바라다
plan to ~~하기를 계획하다

교과서 예문 (p.90, 96)

  • I really want to see the sunrise.
  • I want to buy new sneakers.
  • He hopes to travel the world.
  • She plans to meet her friends.
  • We decided to watch a movie.

고쳐 쓰기 (p.98)

✕ I decided going hiking. → ○ I decided to go hiking.

✕ Do you want join me? → ○ Do you want to join me?

말하기 (p.97)

I want to go to Egypt. I hope to visit the pyramids. (see kangaroos / take pictures in the Colosseum / go up the Eiffel Tower)

I plan to save food. / I want to play every day. / I hope to get some food.

언어 형식 2 · 비인칭 주어 it

해석하지 않는 it p.96~97

나타내는 것질문답
시간What time is it now?It's eleven thirty. / It's 10 o'clock.
요일What day is it today?It's Sunday. / It's Friday.
날짜What date is it today?It's November 1st. / It's October 2nd.
날씨How's the weather now?It's sunny. / It's rainy (raining).
명암(본문)It was dark outside. (p.91)

비인칭 it

앞에 가리킬 명사가 없다. '그것'으로 해석하지 않는다.

It was sunny today. / It was dark outside. (과거는 It was ~)

지시대명사 it (구분!)

앞 문장의 명사를 대신하므로 '그것'으로 해석한다.

Subin and I pushed it. (= 흔들바위) / It was like a pancake. (= 감자전)

날짜 말하기

It's August 2nd. 「월 + 서수」 / 읽을 때는 서수로: September fifth

일기 머리글: Friday, September 5th (요일 + 월 + 날짜) / 달력 July 1일 → It's July 1st. (p.98)

철자 주의

✕ fiveth → ○ fifth

eighth, ninth도 철자를 외우자.

Topic 3 · Fun in Sokcho! ②

9월 6일과 After You Read p.91~92

Saturday, September 6th

  • 일찍 일어남(wake up 과거 woke up), 밖은 dark → 해변으로 달려감
  • 하늘이 천천히(slowly) 붉게 변함(turned red), "해가 떠오른다, 소원을 빌어!" → 눈을 감고 소원을 빌었다(make a wish)
  • 점심은 gamjajeon (like a pancake)
  • 속초 등대 전망대: 가파른 계단(steep stairs, walk up), 다리가 tired, 꼭대기에서 경치(view)는 도시·바다·산 = the city, the ocean, and the mountains
  • 등대는 약 70년(about), 지금도 밤에 배들을 위해 빛을 비춤. 맺음: 모든 순간이 훌륭했고 사진을 많이 찍음, 한국을 언제나(always) 기억

사건 순서 (p.98)

마무리 · 평가 방향 p.93~99

헷갈리기 쉬운 것

  • be going to + V(예정) vs want/hope/plan + to V(바람·계획)
  • 과거형: saw, shook, woke, ran, went, ate, had, put, said, took, made
  • 일기는 과거, 미래 의지는 will(I will always remember Korea.)
  • Subin and I (다른 사람 먼저), Every moment was wonderful

Topic 4 · Meet the World (p.93~95)

  • 글쓰기 THINK → WRITE → REWRITE: I live in ~. There are some cool places in ~. You can ~ there. / come and visit ~. / I hope to see you soon.
  • 쓰기 틀: I live in ~. (동네 소개글 틀) / 메모 예: to hike Hallasan, to play on the beach

언어 형식 2 · 비인칭 주어 it

해석하지 않는 it p.96~97

나타내는 것질문답
시간What time is it now?It's eleven thirty. / It's 10 o'clock.
요일What day is it today?It's Sunday. / It's Friday.
날짜What date is it today?It's November 1st. / It's October 2nd.
날씨How's the weather now?It's sunny. / It's rainy (raining).
명암(본문)It was dark outside. (p.91)

비인칭 it

앞에 가리킬 명사가 없다. '그것'으로 해석하지 않는다.

It was sunny today. / It was dark outside. (과거는 It was ~)

지시대명사 it (구분!)

앞 문장의 명사를 대신하므로 '그것'으로 해석한다.

Subin and I pushed it. (= 흔들바위) / It was like a pancake. (= 감자전)

언어 형식 1 · 부정사(명사적 용법)

목적어 자리의 to부정사 p.96~97

형태와 뜻 (decide, agree, expect도 같은 쓰임)

to + 동사원형이 동사의 목적어로 쓰여 ~하는 것(을), ~하기(로)

동사뜻
want to ~~하기를 원하다
hope to ~~하기를 바라다
plan to ~~하기를 계획하다

교과서 예문 (p.90, 96)

  • I really want to see the sunrise.
  • I want to buy new sneakers.
  • He hopes to travel the world.
  • She plans to meet her friends.
  • We decided to watch a movie.

고쳐 쓰기 (p.98)

✕ I decided going hiking. → ○ I decided to go hiking.

✕ Do you want join me? → ○ Do you want to join me?

말하기 (p.97)

I want to go to Egypt. I hope to visit the pyramids. (see kangaroos / take pictures in the Colosseum / go up the Eiffel Tower)

I plan to save food. / I want to play every day. / I hope to get some food.

핵심 표현

관심 · 장래 희망 말하기 p.98~101

관심 말하기 (p.98)

A What are you interested in?
B I'm interested in solving puzzles. It's challenging.
  • in + 동명사(구)/명사 (전치사 뒤) : playing sports, taking care of animals
  • 이유·느낌 : It's challenging. / exciting. / fun.
  • What are your interests? / My interests are ~.

장래 희망 말하기 (p.99)

A What do you want to be in the future?
B I want to be a doctor.
A Why do you want to be a doctor?
B I want to help sick people.
  • a(n) + 직업 : a doctor / an animal doctor
  • What is your dream job? / My dream job is ~.

언어 형식 ②

시간을 나타내는 접속사 when p.107

의미·형태 · 쓰임

  • 의미 : '~할 때'
  • 형태 : when + 주어 + 동사 (시간을 나타내는 부사절)
  • 교과서 예문 : When Rahul was in middle school, he acted... When I'm sad, I eat peanut ice cream. My dad cooks dinner when he comes home early.
  • 본문·활동 용례 (p.105, p.108) : He takes care of the players when they get hurt. I decided to be a writer when I was in middle school.

참고 규칙 설명 (교사용·지도서)

  • 위치와 쉼표 : 주절 앞 · 뒤 모두 가능. 앞에 오면 콤마(,) — When I cooked dinner, my mom smiled. / My mom smiled when I cooked dinner.
  • 미래의 일 : when절 안은 현재시제. 학생용 예시 I'll be a car designer when I become 28 years old. (p.110)

참고 자주 하는 오류 (교과서에 오류 목록은 없고, 규칙에서 도출한 일반 설명)

  • When I'm sad I eat... (X) → When I'm sad, I eat... · when was in middle school (X) → when Rahul was...
  • When he comes home early. (X, 단독 문장) · When Rahul is in middle school, he acted (X) → was ... acted
  • 접속사 when (I decided ... when I was in middle school) ≠ 의문사 When (When did you decide to do this job? '언제')
